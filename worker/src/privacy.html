Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Jackson Film Club ("we") operates jxnfilm.club and the membership service at join.jxnfilm.club. We're a small film club in Jackson, Mississippi — not a data company. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what we collect and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Email address — for one-time login codes, and (only if you opt in) club announcements.
  • Display name, plus optional pronouns and a Letterboxd username — whatever you choose to show on your member card. The Letterboxd handle is also used to fetch your public Letterboxd activity for the Watched page.
  • RSVP records — when you RSVP to a member-hosted screening, we record your name, email, and the time you RSVP'd, so the host knows who's coming and we can email you the details.
  • Guest RSVPs — a host (or a club admin) can add a non-member guest to their screening's list by name, optionally with an email the guest gave them. A name-only guest has no email stored at all. If an email is given, we send one confirmation that says who added you, with the screening details and a one-click removal link — that's your opt-out, no account needed. Guest names appear in the attendance list the same way member names do.
  • Host addresses — if you host a house screening, you give us the street address and any notes (parking, entry). The address is private: it appears only in confirmation emails to confirmed RSVPs and in your own host view — never on the public site or in any public data.

Where it lives

Your email, RSVP records, and host addresses are stored privately in Cloudflare Workers KV. Your email is never published in the public members list or committed to the public GitHub repository. What is public: your display name, pronouns, Letterboxd handle, and which screenings you attended (names only) — on the site and in the repository's public data/*.json files.

How we use your email

  • One-time login codes when you sign in.
  • Screening emails you asked for: RSVP confirmations (these include the host's address), and updates or cancellations for screenings you've RSVP'd to.
  • Club announcements — only if you opt in, via the checkbox at signup or the toggle on your account page. Every announcement includes a one-click unsubscribe link and the club's postal address (as CAN-SPAM requires). Opting out never affects login codes or RSVP emails.

We never sell, rent, or share your email with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies and run no analytics or trackers. Three small entries in your browser's localStorage (jxnfc_session, jxnfc_otp_inflight, jxnfc_privacy_ack) keep you signed in and remember which version of this policy you've already seen; they stay on your device, and the sign-in entries are cleared when you sign out. One exception worth naming: the homepage embeds our podcast via a Spotify player, and Spotify sets its own cookies inside that frame — see Spotify's privacy policy.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts the membership service and its storage. Its standard infrastructure logs record request metadata (like IP addresses) under Cloudflare's own retention.
  • Resend delivers our email. We don't keep contact lists there — it only sees each message as it's sent.
  • GitHub hosts the public site and the public data files.
  • TMDB serves the film poster images your browser loads.
  • Letterboxd is the source of the public film activity we show; we only read what you've already made public there.
  • Spotify powers the podcast player on the homepage (see above).

How long we keep things

  • Login codes expire after 10 minutes; sign-in sessions after 1 hour. If you tick Remember my login on this device at sign-in, that device stays signed in for up to 30 days of inactivity — signing out (or deleting your membership) revokes it immediately.
  • 30 days after a screening, we automatically delete its RSVP list (the emails — guest emails included) and the host's address and notes. What stays is the names-only attendance history — the club's record of who came.
  • Your email stays as long as your membership does, and goes when you delete it (below).
  • Our newsletter log keeps only the subject line and recipient count — never addresses.

Your choices

  • Delete your membership yourself, instantly — the danger zone on your account page removes your email, profile, and upcoming RSVPs on the spot. Optionally, you can also anonymize your name in past attendance lists.
  • Unsubscribe from announcements — one click from any announcement email, or the toggle on your account page.
  • Cancel an RSVP — one click from the confirmation email, or from the event page.
  • Added as a guest and don't want to be? — one click on the removal link in the confirmation email takes you off the list, and the 30-day deletion above erases the email record either way. The host can also remove you on request.
  • Questions, or want us to do any of this for you? Email privacy@jxnfilm.club.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and post a notice on jxnfilm.club.
